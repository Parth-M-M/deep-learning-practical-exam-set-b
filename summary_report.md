# Deep Learning Practical Exam (Set B) — Summary Report

## 1. Overview of the Five Tasks and Datasets Used
As a Junior Deep Learning Engineer building foundational AI pipelines across healthcare, fashion-retail, and e-commerce domains, I implemented and evaluated five self-contained mini-projects:

1. **Step 1 — Artificial Neural Network (ANN) for Clinical Tabular Classification:** Using the **Breast Cancer Wisconsin (Diagnostic) Dataset** (`569` patient biopsy records across `30` numeric cell-nuclei features, split 80/20 with stratification and standardized via `StandardScaler`), I built a 3-layer Keras Sequential ANN (`Dense(32, ReLU) → Dropout(0.3) → Dense(16, ReLU) → Dense(1, Sigmoid)`) to classify tumours as Malignant (`0`) or Benign (`1`).
2. **Step 2 — OpenCV Image Preprocessing & Feature Extraction Pipeline:** Using **5 high-contrast object images** (`Coin`, `Fruit/Apple`, `Botanical Leaf`, `Mobile Phone`, and `Hardcover Book`), I constructed a deterministic computer-vision pipeline performing `200×200` area-interpolated resizing, BGR-to-Grayscale conversion, $5\times5$ Median Blur denoising, automatic Otsu binary segmentation, external contour extraction, BGR channel histogram computation (`cv2.calcHist`), and geometric augmentation (`cv2.flip` and $45^\circ$ `cv2.warpAffine` rotation).
3. **Step 3 — Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) for Fashion-Retail Classification:** Using a subset of **Fashion-MNIST** (`20,000` training and `4,000` test grayscale $28\times28$ images across `10` apparel categories), I trained a two-block CNN (`Conv2D(32) → MaxPool2D → Conv2D(64) → MaxPool2D → Flatten → Dense(128) → Dropout(0.3) → Dense(10, Softmax)`).
4. **Step 4 — Recurrent Neural Network (LSTM & SimpleRNN) for Text Sentiment Classification:** Using the **IMDB Movie Reviews Dataset** (`10,000` training and `5,000` test reviews restricted to the top `10,000` words and padded/truncated to `maxlen = 100`), I trained an `Embedding(10000, 32) → LSTM(32) → Dense(1, Sigmoid)` model and benchmarked it against a `SimpleRNN(32)` baseline.
5. **Step 5 — Pre-trained Hugging Face Transformers for E-Commerce NLP:** Using **20 custom Flipkart/Amazon product reviews** (`8` positive, `8` negative, `4` neutral/mixed, plus `3` code-mixed Hinglish reviews) and **5 Indian/global business sentences**, I evaluated zero-training sentiment classification (`cardiffnlp/twitter-roberta-base-sentiment-latest`), WordPiece subword tokenization (`distilbert-base-uncased`), and Named Entity Recognition (`dslim/bert-base-NER`).

## 2. Key Deep Learning Concepts Learned
Comparing **WordPiece subword tokenization and Self-Attention in Transformers** against integer-indexed **LSTM sequence modeling** was the most illuminating concept. In Step 4, any word outside the top 10,000 vocabulary collapsed into an uninformative `<UNK>` token, and `SimpleRNN(32)` suffered a 7.06% accuracy drop relative to `LSTM(32)` due to vanishing gradients over 100 time steps. By contrast, in Step 5, `distilbert-base-uncased` decomposed unseen domain words like `Flipkart` (`['flip', '##kar', '##t']`) and `overheating` (`['over', '##heat', '##ing']`) into meaningful subword morphemes, while RoBERTa's self-attention weighed contrastive clauses (*"however the battery drains quickly"*) across the entire sentence in parallel.

## 3. Summary of Results Achieved
- **Step 1 (ANN — Breast Cancer):** Achieved **97.37% Test Accuracy** (`111/114` correct), **97.26% Precision**, **98.61% Recall**, and **97.93% F1-Score** in 40 epochs with `1,537` trainable parameters.
- **Step 2 (OpenCV Pipeline):** Segmented all **5/5 objects** cleanly using Otsu's method (thresholds `143.0–199.0`), extracting primary object contour areas ranging from **7,840.0 px²** (Leaf) to **14,254.0 px²** (Book).
- **Step 3 (CNN — Fashion-MNIST):** Achieved **89.45% Test Accuracy** and **89.44% Macro F1** after 8 epochs (`225,034` parameters), identifying `Shirt` vs `T-shirt/top` as the primary confusion pair.
- **Step 4 (RNN — IMDB Reviews):** Achieved **83.24% Test Accuracy** with `LSTM(32)` (`328,353` parameters), outperforming the `SimpleRNN(32)` baseline (**76.18%**) by **+7.06 percentage points**.
- **Step 5 (Transformers — Sentiment & NER):** Achieved **100.0% (16/16)** accuracy on clearly positive/negative reviews (confidence `0.9540–0.9914`), appropriately lower confidence (`0.5420–0.7415`) on ambiguous mixed reviews, and extracted **16/16 named entities** (`PER`, `ORG`, `LOC`, `MISC`).

## 4. Next Steps with More Time and Compute
Given additional compute and time, I would: (1) upgrade the Fashion-MNIST CNN with `BatchNormalization`, residual skip connections, and random horizontal flip/cutout augmentation on all 60,000 training images to resolve `Shirt` vs `T-shirt/top` ambiguity; (2) replace the unidirectional IMDB `LSTM(32)` with a **Bidirectional LSTM (`Bidirectional(LSTM(64))`)** initialized with pre-trained GloVe embeddings; and (3) fine-tune an Indian multilingual Transformer (**`ai4bharat/IndicBERTv2`** or **`google/muril-base-cased`**) on code-mixed Hinglish e-commerce reviews and export it to quantized **ONNX Runtime** for low-latency production serving.
